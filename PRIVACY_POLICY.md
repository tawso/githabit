# Privacy Policy for GitHabit

**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

GitHabit ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the GitHabit mobile application.

---

### 1. Zero Tracking & Local-First Philosophy
* GitHabit is designed with a **privacy-first, local-first architecture**.
* Your habit checklists, Pomodoro sessions, scratchpad notes, and streaks are stored **locally on your device** using embedded Hive storage.
* We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.
* We do **not** use third-party analytics trackers or advertising SDKs.

---

### 2. External Services & APIs
When you choose to connect third-party developer platforms (such as GitHub, LeetCode, or WakaTime):
* **GitHub Integration:** The App accesses publicly available GitHub user contributions and activity via the GitHub public API. If you provide a GitHub Personal Access Token (PAT), it is securely stored locally on your device and used solely to communicate directly with GitHub servers.
* **LeetCode & WakaTime:** Used only to fetch your personal coding and problem-solving statistics directly from their respective APIs.

---

### 3. Device Permissions
* **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used strictly for querying GitHub, LeetCode, and WakaTime APIs to synchronize coding activity.
* **Network State (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`):** Used to check connectivity before making API requests.
* **Vibrate (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used exclusively for tactile haptic feedback during habit interactions and Pomodoro timer completions.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion
Since your data resides on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing application storage immediately deletes all stored habits, streaks, and cached tokens.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
GitHabit is intended for developers and students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---
